# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**THIS RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT** ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 15, 2025, by and between:

**LESSOR:** Cascade Property Holdings LLC, an Oregon limited liability company, whose principal address is 1800 NW Murray Road, Suite 204, Portland, Oregon 97229 ("Lessor"), and

**LESSEE:** [Lessee Name(s)] ("Lessee").

The parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. PREMISES DESCRIPTION

Lessor hereby leases to Lessee the residential dwelling located at:

**742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 3B, Portland, Oregon 97201**

("Premises"), together with the following appurtenances: one (1) assigned parking space designated as Space #47, access to the shared laundry facilities on the ground floor, and use of the communal courtyard area as described in the Building Rules and Regulations attached hereto as **Exhibit A**.

The Premises comprises approximately 875 square feet, consisting of two (2) bedrooms, one (1) full bathroom, a kitchen, and a living/dining area. The Premises is located on the third floor of the building known as Evergreen Terrace Apartments.

Lessee acknowledges that Lessee has inspected the Premises prior to execution of this Agreement and accepts the Premises in its current "as-is" condition, subject to any items specifically noted in the Move-In Condition Checklist attached as **Exhibit B**, which Lessor agrees to remedy within thirty (30) days of the Commencement Date.

---

## 2. LEASE TERM

### 2.1 Initial Term

This Agreement shall commence on **August 1, 2025** ("Commencement Date") and shall terminate on **July 31, 2026** ("Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions hereof (the "Lease Term").

### 2.2 Holdover

If Lessee remains in possession of the Premises after the Expiration Date without the express written consent of Lessor, Lessee shall be deemed a month-to-month tenant at a monthly rent equal to one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the rent then in effect, subject to all other terms and conditions of this Agreement. Lessor may terminate such holdover tenancy upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

### 2.3 Renewal Option

Lessee shall have the option to renew this Agreement for one (1) additional twelve-month term upon written notice delivered to Lessor no fewer than sixty (60) days prior to the Expiration Date, subject to Lessor's approval and a rent adjustment not to exceed five percent (5%) of the then-current monthly rent.

---

## 3. RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT

### 3.1 Monthly Rent

Lessee agrees to pay Lessor the sum of **Two Thousand One Hundred Fifty Dollars ($2,150.00)** per month ("Rent"), payable in advance on the first (1st) day of each calendar month during the Lease Term.

### 3.2 Prorated Rent

If the Commencement Date falls on a day other than the first day of a calendar month, Rent for such partial month shall be prorated on a per-diem basis (Rent divided by 30).

### 3.3 Late Charge

Rent not received by Lessor by the fifth (5th) day of the month shall be subject to a late charge of Seventy-Five Dollars ($75.00). If Rent remains unpaid after the tenth (10th) day of the month, an additional late charge of Twenty-Five Dollars ($25.00) per day shall accrue until paid in full. The parties agree such charges represent a reasonable estimate of the costs incurred by Lessor as a result of late payment and do not constitute a penalty.

### 3.4 Method of Payment

All Rent payments shall be made by electronic funds transfer (ACH), cashier's check, or money order payable to Cascade Property Holdings LLC. Personal checks are accepted only during the first three (3) months of tenancy. Cash payments will not be accepted.

### 3.5 Security Deposit

Upon execution of this Agreement, Lessee shall deposit with Lessor the sum of **Four Thousand Three Hundred Dollars ($4,300.00)** ("Security Deposit"). The Security Deposit is equivalent to two (2) months' Rent and is held as security for Lessee's faithful performance of all obligations under this Agreement.

### 3.6 Return of Security Deposit

Lessor shall return the Security Deposit, less any amounts properly withheld, within **thirty-one (31) days** following the later of: (a) the termination of this Agreement, or (b) Lessee's surrender of the Premises, together with a written itemization of any deductions. Deductions may be made for unpaid Rent, damage to the Premises beyond ordinary wear and tear, unreturned keys, and cleaning costs if the Premises is not returned in substantially the same condition as received.

### 3.7 Non-Refundable Administrative Fee

In addition to the Security Deposit, Lessee shall pay a non-refundable administrative fee of Two Hundred Fifty Dollars ($250.00) upon execution of this Agreement. Such fee covers credit screening, lease preparation, and move-in coordination.

---

## 4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

### 4.1 Lessor's Obligations

Lessor shall maintain the Premises in a habitable condition and shall be responsible for:

(a) Structural components, including roof, exterior walls, foundation, and common areas;

(b) Plumbing systems, including supply lines, drain lines, and fixtures, except where damage is caused by Lessee's negligence or misuse;

(c) Electrical systems, including the main panel and all permanently installed fixtures;

(d) Heating and ventilation systems ("HVAC"), including furnace, heat pump, and central air conditioning equipment;

(e) Appliances provided with the Premises at the Commencement Date, including the refrigerator, dishwasher, range/oven, and in-unit washer/dryer connections;

(f) Pest extermination services, where infestation is not caused by Lessee's acts or omissions;

(g) Common area maintenance, including hallways, laundry facilities, elevator, and exterior grounds.

Lessee shall notify Lessor in writing (email to maintenance@cascadepropertypdx.com or via the online portal) of any condition requiring repair. Lessor shall respond to emergency repairs within **twenty-four (24) hours** and non-emergency repairs within **fourteen (14) calendar days**.

### 4.2 Lessee's Obligations

Lessee shall maintain the Premises in a clean, sanitary, and orderly condition and shall be responsible for:

(a) Replacing light bulbs, batteries in smoke detectors and carbon monoxide detectors, and HVAC air filters (replaced every 90 days);

(b) Minor repairs costing less than Fifty Dollars ($50.00), including unclogging drains, tightening loose hinges, and replacing broken switch plates;

(c) Maintaining the Premises free of trash, debris, and conditions that may attract pests;

(d) Reporting any water leaks, mold, or moisture intrusion within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery;

(e) Repairing or paying for repair of any damage to the Premises, fixtures, or appliances caused by Lessee, Lessee's guests, or Lessee's pets beyond ordinary wear and tear.

### 4.3 Alterations

Lessee shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of Lessor. Approved alterations shall, at Lessor's election, either remain with the Premises upon termination or be removed by Lessee at Lessee's expense, restoring the Premises to its original condition.

---

## 5. PET POLICY

### 5.1 Permitted Pets

Subject to the terms of this Section, Lessee is permitted to keep household pets in the Premises, subject to the following limitations:

(a) **Maximum number of pets:** Two (2) pets per unit;

(b) **Weight limit:** No individual pet shall exceed **fifty pounds (50 lbs)** at adult weight;

(c) **Permitted species:** Dogs and cats only. Reptiles, birds, exotic animals, and livestock are not permitted;

(d) **Breed restrictions:** The following breeds (and mixes thereof) are not permitted: American Pit Bull Terrier, Rottweiler, Doberman Pinscher, Chow Chow, Akita, and Wolf Hybrid.

### 5.2 Pet Deposit

For each approved pet, Lessee shall pay a refundable pet deposit of **Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00) per pet**, due at the time of Lessor's written approval. The pet deposit is held separately from the Security Deposit and is subject to the same return procedures described in Section 3.6.

### 5.3 Pet Addendum

Lessee must complete and execute the Pet Addendum (Exhibit C) for each approved pet, providing veterinary records, current vaccination status, and a current photograph of the animal. Failure to disclose a pet constitutes a material breach of this Agreement.

### 5.4 Pet-Related Damage

Lessee shall be liable for all damage caused by any pet, including but not limited to carpet replacement, door/baseboard scratching, and odor remediation. Lessor reserves the right to require professional cleaning of carpets and air ducts upon termination where pets were present.

---

## 6. UTILITIES

### 6.1 Lessee-Paid Utilities

Lessee shall be solely responsible for establishing service accounts and paying all charges for the following utilities:

(a) **Electricity** — Portland General Electric (PGE); Lessee must establish account prior to Commencement Date;

(b) **Natural gas** — NW Natural; Lessee must establish account prior to Commencement Date;

(c) **Internet and cable television** — Lessee's choice of provider; Comcast Xfinity and CenturyLink serve the building;

(d) **Renter's insurance** — Lessee is required to maintain a renter's insurance policy with minimum liability coverage of One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) throughout the Lease Term. Proof of coverage must be provided to Lessor within fifteen (15) days of the Commencement Date.

### 6.2 Lessor-Paid Utilities

Lessor shall pay for the following utilities as part of the monthly Rent:

(a) **Water and sewer** — City of Portland Water Bureau;

(b) **Trash and recycling** — Metro solid waste collection, including curbside recycling and compost;

(c) **Common area electricity and gas** — including hallway lighting, lobby heating, elevator, and exterior lighting.

### 6.3 Excessive Consumption

In the event that Lessee's water consumption, as measured by the individual unit submeter, exceeds One Thousand (1,000) gallons per month, Lessor reserves the right to assess a surcharge of $0.015 per gallon for all consumption above such threshold.

---

## 7. EARLY TERMINATION

### 7.1 Notice Requirement

Lessee may terminate this Agreement prior to the Expiration Date by providing Lessor with written notice of no fewer than **sixty (60) calendar days** prior to the intended date of early termination ("Early Termination Date"). Notice must be delivered via certified mail or hand-delivered to Lessor's office during business hours.

### 7.2 Early Termination Penalty

In addition to the notice required under Section 7.1, Lessee shall pay to Lessor an early termination fee equal to **two (2) months' Rent** (i.e., $4,300.00) as liquidated damages, not as a penalty, representing a reasonable estimate of Lessor's re-leasing costs. This fee shall be due and payable at the time the early termination notice is delivered.

### 7.3 Mitigation

Notwithstanding Section 7.2, Lessor shall make commercially reasonable efforts to re-let the Premises following early termination. To the extent Lessor secures a replacement tenant with a commencement date that falls within the original Lease Term, the early termination fee shall be reduced on a pro-rata basis for the period covered by the replacement tenancy.

### 7.4 Exceptions

The early termination penalty shall be waived in the following circumstances:

(a) Lessee is a member of the United States Armed Forces and receives qualifying deployment orders or a permanent change of station, provided Lessee delivers a copy of such orders with the termination notice;

(b) Lessee is a victim of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking as defined under Oregon Revised Statutes Chapter 90, provided Lessee delivers appropriate documentation with the termination notice;

(c) Lessor materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the applicable notice period.

---

## 8. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT

### 8.1 Prohibition Without Consent

Lessee shall not sublet the Premises or any portion thereof, nor assign this Agreement or any interest therein, without the prior written approval of Lessor, which approval may be withheld in Lessor's reasonable discretion.

### 8.2 Subletting Request Process

To request approval for subletting, Lessee must submit to Lessor: (a) the full legal name and contact information of the proposed sublessee; (b) a completed rental application on Lessor's standard form; (c) proof of income demonstrating the proposed sublessee's monthly income is at least three times (3x) the monthly Rent; and (d) Lessee's written statement of the proposed subletting term and reason therefor.

Lessor shall respond to a complete subletting request within fourteen (14) business days. Failure to respond shall not be deemed approval.

### 8.3 Lessee Remains Liable

Approval of a subletting arrangement does not release Lessee from any obligation under this Agreement. Lessee remains fully liable for all Rent, damages, and other obligations through the Expiration Date, regardless of any subletting arrangement.

### 8.4 Short-Term Rentals

Lessee expressly agrees not to list the Premises on any short-term rental platform, including but not limited to Airbnb, VRBO, or similar services, whether for individual rooms or the entire Premises.

---

## 9. PARKING

### 9.1 Assigned Parking Space

Lessor hereby assigns to Lessee the exclusive use of parking space number **#47** in the surface lot adjacent to the building, at no additional charge. Parking Space #47 is designated for one (1) passenger vehicle only. Lessee shall not store inoperable vehicles, trailers, recreational vehicles, or commercial vehicles in the assigned space.

### 9.2 Additional Parking

If Lessee requires additional parking, Lessee may rent additional surface lot spaces (subject to availability) at the rate of **Seventy-Five Dollars ($75.00) per month per space**, payable as additional Rent. Such additional spaces are offered on a month-to-month basis and may be terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

### 9.3 Guest Parking

Guest parking is available in the unmarked visitor spaces located at the east end of the surface lot. Guest parking is limited to forty-eight (48) consecutive hours per visit. Vehicles parked in violation of these rules are subject to towing at the vehicle owner's expense.

### 9.4 Parking Rules

Lessee and Lessee's guests shall comply with all posted parking regulations. Vehicles leaking oil or other fluids on the property are subject to immediate towing.
