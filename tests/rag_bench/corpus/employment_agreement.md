# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

**This Employment Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between **Meridian Dynamics Corporation**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1400 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("Company"), and **Jordan A. Whitfield** ("Employee"), residing at the address on file with the Company's Human Resources department.

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ Employee on the terms and conditions set forth herein; and

WHEREAS, Employee desires to accept such employment on those terms and conditions;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, promises, and obligations set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Definitions

For purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

**1.1 "Affiliate"** means any entity that, directly or indirectly through one or more intermediaries, controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with, the Company, where "control" means the possession, direct or indirect, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of such entity, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.

**1.2 "Board"** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

**1.3 "Cause"** means: (a) Employee's willful failure to perform, or gross negligence in the performance of, Employee's material duties and responsibilities; (b) Employee's conviction of, or plea of nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude; (c) Employee's commission of fraud, embezzlement, misappropriation, or material dishonesty in connection with the Company or its Affiliates; (d) Employee's material breach of this Agreement, any Company policy, or any fiduciary duty owed to the Company, which breach, if curable, remains uncured for thirty (30) days following written notice from the Company; or (e) Employee's material violation of any applicable law or regulation relating to the Company's business.

**1.4 "Change in Control"** means: (a) a merger, consolidation, or reorganization of the Company in which the Company's stockholders immediately prior to such transaction own less than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting securities of the surviving entity; (b) the sale or disposition of all or substantially all of the Company's assets; or (c) the acquisition by any person or group of persons of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the total combined voting power of the Company's outstanding securities.

**1.5 "Confidential Information"** means all information and data of a confidential, proprietary, or trade secret nature relating to the Company or its Affiliates, including but not limited to: technical data, trade secrets, know-how, research and development data, product plans, products, services, client lists, markets, software, source code, developments, inventions, processes, formulas, technology, designs, drawings, engineering, hardware configuration, marketing, finances, business strategies, and other business information disclosed to Employee either directly or indirectly in writing, orally, or by drawings or inspection of parts or equipment, or that Employee creates or discovers in the course of employment.

**1.6 "Disability"** means Employee's inability to substantially perform the essential functions of Employee's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for a continuous period of ninety (90) days or for one hundred eighty (180) days in any twelve (12) month period, as determined by a qualified physician selected by mutual agreement of the parties.

**1.7 "Good Reason"** means, without Employee's prior written consent: (a) a material reduction in Employee's base compensation; (b) a material diminution in Employee's duties, authority, or responsibilities; (c) a requirement that Employee relocate Employee's principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles; or (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company that remains uncured for thirty (30) days following Employee's written notice to the Company.

**1.8 "Intellectual Property"** has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1 of this Agreement.

**1.9 "Restricted Territory"** means the United States and any other geographic region in which the Company or its Affiliates conducted business during the twenty-four (24) months preceding the cessation of Employee's employment.

**1.10 "Term"** means the period commencing on the Effective Date and continuing until Employee's employment with the Company is terminated in accordance with Section 7 of this Agreement.

## 2. Position and Duties

**2.1 Position.** The Company agrees to employ Employee as **Vice President of Product Engineering**, and Employee agrees to serve in such capacity, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement. Employee shall report directly to the Chief Technology Officer ("CTO"), or such other executive officer as the Board may designate from time to time.

**2.2 Principal Place of Employment.** Employee's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices located at 1400 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801, subject to reasonable travel requirements inherent in Employee's position. The Company reserves the right to require Employee to work remotely or from alternate locations as reasonably necessary for business purposes.

**2.3 Duties and Responsibilities.** Employee shall have the following primary duties and responsibilities, which may be modified from time to time by the CTO or the Board at their reasonable discretion:

(a) Provide strategic and technical leadership for the Company's product engineering division, comprising approximately forty-five (45) full-time engineers across five (5) functional teams;

(b) Define, communicate, and execute the product engineering roadmap in alignment with the Company's broader strategic objectives;

(c) Collaborate with Product Management, Design, Quality Assurance, and Customer Success stakeholders to ensure engineering deliverables meet functional and non-functional requirements;

(d) Establish and maintain engineering standards, operational procedures, architectural guidelines, and best practices;

(e) Manage the engineering division's annual budget, currently projected at $8.2 million for fiscal year 2026, including headcount, tooling, infrastructure, and third-party vendor expenditures;

(f) Recruit, develop, mentor, and retain engineering talent, including conducting performance evaluations and compensation reviews;

(g) Represent the Company's engineering capabilities to customers, partners, investors, and other external stakeholders as reasonably requested; and

(h) Such other duties and responsibilities as may be assigned from time to time by the CTO or the Board, consistent with Employee's position and expertise.

**2.4 Exclusivity of Service.** During the Term, Employee shall devote Employee's full business time, attention, skill, and efforts exclusively to the business and affairs of the Company and its Affiliates. Employee shall not, during the Term, directly or indirectly engage in any business, enterprise, or activity that competes with or is adverse to the Company's interests without the prior written consent of the Board. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Employee may engage in the following activities provided they do not materially interfere with Employee's duties hereunder: (a) personal investment activities; (b) civic, charitable, and non-profit board service; and (c) up to two (2) outside corporate board positions, subject to prior written approval from the Board.

**2.5 Code of Conduct and Policies.** Employee agrees to comply with all Company policies, procedures, and codes of conduct as may be in effect from time to time, including the Company's Code of Business Conduct and Ethics, Information Security Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, and Anti-Harassment Policy, copies of which have been provided to Employee.

## 3. Compensation and Benefits

**3.1 Base Compensation.** In consideration for Employee's services hereunder, the Company shall pay Employee an annual base compensation of **One Hundred Forty-Five Thousand Dollars ($145,000)** (the "Base Compensation"), payable in accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices and subject to applicable withholdings. The Base Compensation shall be reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee of the Board or its designee, with any adjustments to become effective on or about January 1 of each year. Nothing in this Section shall be construed as a guarantee of any increase in Base Compensation.

**3.2 Incentive Compensation.** Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company's Management Incentive Compensation Program (the "MICP"), with a target incentive compensation opportunity equal to **forty percent (40%)** of Base Compensation per calendar year, subject to the achievement of performance targets established by the Compensation Committee. Incentive compensation under the MICP, if earned, shall be paid no later than March 15 following the close of each applicable calendar year, and shall be prorated for any partial year of employment. The specific metrics, weightings, and payout schedules applicable to Employee shall be set forth in a separate written MICP participation agreement provided to Employee annually. The right to receive any incentive compensation payment is conditioned upon Employee's continued employment through the applicable payment date, unless otherwise provided in Section 7 of this Agreement.

**3.3 Equity Compensation.** Subject to approval by the Board and execution of the Company's standard Restricted Stock Unit Agreement ("RSU Agreement"), Employee shall be awarded restricted stock units ("RSUs") representing the right to receive shares of the Company's common stock with an aggregate grant-date value of **Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000)**, calculated using the Company's most recent 409A valuation as of the Effective Date. The RSUs shall vest over a four (4) year period, with twenty-five percent (25%) vesting on the first anniversary of the grant date and the remainder vesting in equal quarterly installments thereafter, subject to Employee's continued employment and the terms of the RSU Agreement. In the event of a Change in Control, unvested RSUs shall accelerate and vest in full, subject to Employee's continued employment through the closing date of such transaction or as otherwise provided in the RSU Agreement.

**3.4 Health and Welfare Benefits.** During the Term, Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company's standard health and welfare benefit plans, including:

(a) **Medical Insurance**: Comprehensive group medical coverage for Employee and eligible dependents, with the Company contributing ninety percent (90%) of the applicable premium cost and Employee responsible for the remaining ten percent (10%);

(b) **Dental Insurance**: Group dental coverage for Employee and eligible dependents, with the Company contributing eighty percent (80%) of the applicable premium cost;

(c) **Vision Insurance**: Group vision coverage for Employee and eligible dependents, with the Company contributing seventy-five percent (75%) of the applicable premium cost;

(d) **Life Insurance**: Group term life insurance in an amount equal to two (2) times Employee's annual Base Compensation, at no cost to Employee;

(e) **Long-Term Disability**: Group long-term disability insurance providing a monthly benefit of up to sixty percent (60%) of Employee's monthly Base Compensation in the event of Disability, at no cost to Employee; and

(f) **Supplemental Benefits**: Access to the Company's Employee Assistance Program, legal services plan, and identity protection services.

Participation in all benefit plans is subject to the terms of the applicable plan documents, which may be amended from time to time. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a plan document, the plan document shall control with respect to benefit entitlements.

**3.5 Retirement Plan.** Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company's 401(k) Savings Plan (the "401(k) Plan") in accordance with its terms. The Company shall match Employee's elective deferrals at a rate of **one hundred percent (100%)** of the first three percent (3%) of eligible compensation deferred, and **fifty percent (50%)** of the next two percent (2%) of eligible compensation deferred, for a maximum Company match of four percent (4%) of eligible compensation per year, subject to applicable IRS limits. Company matching contributions shall vest according to the schedule set forth in the 401(k) Plan document.

**3.6 Paid Time Off.** Employee shall be entitled to **twenty-five (25) days** of paid time off ("PTO") per calendar year, to be accrued in accordance with the Company's standard PTO policy. Employee is also entitled to observe all Company-designated holidays. PTO accruals in excess of thirty (30) days shall not carry forward to the subsequent calendar year. Upon termination of employment for any reason, accrued but unused PTO shall be paid out in a lump sum at Employee's then-current daily Base Compensation rate, to the extent required by applicable law.

**3.7 Professional Development.** The Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable and documented professional development expenses, including conference attendance, certification programs, and technical training, up to **Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000)** per calendar year, subject to prior written approval and submission of supporting documentation in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policy.

**3.8 Expense Reimbursement.** The Company shall reimburse Employee for all reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred by Employee in the performance of Employee's duties, including travel, lodging, meals, and entertainment, in accordance with the Company's Expense Reimbursement Policy as may be in effect from time to time. All reimbursements are contingent upon timely submission of proper documentation.

## 4. Intellectual Property Assignment

**4.1 Assignment of Inventions.** Employee hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company, its successors, and assigns, Employee's entire right, title, and interest in and to all inventions, discoveries, designs, developments, improvements, processes, formulas, methods, algorithms, software (including source code and object code), databases, documentation, works of authorship, mask works, trade secrets, and any other intellectual property (collectively, "Intellectual Property") that Employee makes, conceives, creates, develops, reduces to practice, or contributes to, either alone or jointly with others, during the Term, that: (a) relate to the Company's actual or demonstrably anticipated business, research, or development activities; (b) result from or are suggested by work performed by Employee for the Company; or (c) are developed using the Company's resources, equipment, facilities, or Confidential Information. Employee's obligation to assign Intellectual Property shall apply whether or not such Intellectual Property is patentable, copyrightable, or eligible for any other form of legal protection.

**4.2 Works Made for Hire.** To the extent any Intellectual Property constitutes a "work made for hire" within the meaning of the Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. § 101, Employee acknowledges and agrees that such work is and shall be the exclusive property of the Company from the moment of creation.

**4.3 Further Assurances.** During and after the Term, Employee shall promptly disclose all Intellectual Property to the Company and shall execute and deliver such additional documents, instruments, and agreements as the Company may reasonably request to perfect, register, or enforce the Company's rights in such Intellectual Property, including executing patent applications, copyright registrations, and assignment documents. The Company shall bear all costs associated with such filings.

**4.4 Prior Inventions.** Attached hereto as **Exhibit A** is a list of all inventions, original works of authorship, developments, concepts, improvements, designs, discoveries, ideas, trademarks, or trade secrets which Employee has, alone or jointly with others, conceived, developed, or reduced to practice prior to the Effective Date, which Employee desires to exclude from the scope of this Section 4 (the "Prior Inventions"). If no such list is attached, Employee represents that there are no Prior Inventions.

**4.5 License to Prior Inventions.** If in the course of employment Employee incorporates any Prior Invention into a Company product, process, or service, Employee hereby grants to the Company a nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable, worldwide license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and sublicense such Prior Invention solely to the extent necessary for the Company's business operations.

**4.6 Power of Attorney.** Employee hereby irrevocably designates and appoints the Company as Employee's attorney-in-fact and agent to act for and on behalf of Employee to execute and file any documents and do all other lawfully permitted acts to further the purposes of this Section 4 with the same legal force and effect as if executed by Employee, in the event Employee becomes legally incapacitated or fails to execute required documents within ten (10) business days of a written request.

## 5. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation

**5.1 Non-Competition.** During the Term and for a period of **eighteen (18) months** following the cessation of Employee's employment for any reason (the "Restricted Period"), Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, within the Restricted Territory, own, manage, operate, control, be employed by, consult for, participate in, or be connected with any business, enterprise, or activity that competes with the Company's business as conducted during the twelve (12) months preceding the cessation of Employee's employment. For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies to Employee serving in any capacity for a Competitive Business, including as an officer, director, employee, partner, consultant, contractor, agent, or investor holding more than two percent (2%) of the outstanding voting securities.

**5.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, on behalf of Employee or any other person or entity: (a) solicit, recruit, or induce any person who is then employed by the Company or its Affiliates to terminate such employment; (b) hire or engage any person who was employed by the Company or its Affiliates within the twelve (12) months preceding such solicitation or engagement; or (c) assist any third party in the foregoing activities.

**5.3 Non-Solicitation of Customers and Partners.** During a period of **twenty-four (24) months** following the cessation of Employee's employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, on behalf of Employee or any third party, solicit, induce, or attempt to induce any customer, prospective customer, partner, vendor, or supplier of the Company or its Affiliates with whom Employee had material contact during the twenty-four (24) months preceding the cessation of employment to: (a) cease or reduce its business relationship with the Company; (b) divert any business from the Company to any competitor; or (c) otherwise take any action that is materially adverse to the Company's business interests.

**5.4 Acknowledgment of Reasonableness.** Employee acknowledges that the foregoing restrictions are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests, including its Confidential Information, trade secrets, customer relationships, and goodwill. Employee further acknowledges that the restrictions will not preclude Employee from engaging in a gainful occupation or earning a livelihood in the industry.

**5.5 Garden Leave.** In connection with the non-competition restriction set forth in Section 5.1, the Company shall pay Employee an amount equal to Employee's monthly Base Compensation rate for each month of the Restricted Period during which the non-compete restriction is enforced, provided that such obligation shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any compensation received by Employee from new employment during such period.

## 6. Confidentiality Obligations

**6.1 Non-Disclosure.** Employee acknowledges that in the course of employment, Employee will receive, create, use, or have access to Confidential Information that is of great value to the Company and its Affiliates. Employee agrees that during the Term and for a period of **five (5) years** following the cessation of Employee's employment for any reason (the "Confidentiality Period"), Employee shall not, directly or indirectly: (a) disclose, reveal, or communicate any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company; (b) use any Confidential Information for Employee's own benefit or for the benefit of any third party; or (c) permit any third party to access or use any Confidential Information.

**6.2 Trade Secrets.** Notwithstanding Section 6.1, Employee's obligations with respect to information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law shall survive the expiration of the Confidentiality Period and shall continue for so long as such information retains its trade secret status.

**6.3 Permitted Disclosures.** Notwithstanding Section 6.1, Employee may disclose Confidential Information: (a) to the extent required by applicable law, regulation, or court order, provided that Employee provides the Company with prompt advance written notice of such requirement and reasonably cooperates with the Company in seeking a protective order or other available relief; (b) to Employee's legal counsel in connection with obtaining legal advice regarding Employee's obligations hereunder; or (c) to the extent such information has become publicly available through no act or omission of Employee.

**6.4 Return of Materials.** Upon the cessation of Employee's employment for any reason, or at any time upon the Company's written request, Employee shall promptly return to the Company all Confidential Information and all other property of the Company in Employee's possession or control, including all documents, files, electronic data, equipment, access credentials, and copies thereof. Employee shall certify in writing that all such materials have been returned or destroyed as directed.

**6.5 Whistleblower Protection.** Nothing in this Agreement shall prohibit Employee from: (a) reporting possible violations of law to the Securities and Exchange Commission or other governmental agencies; (b) making disclosures protected under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or other applicable whistleblower protection statutes; or (c) participating in any governmental investigation or proceeding.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 Termination for Cause.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment immediately for Cause upon written notice to Employee. In the event of termination for Cause, Employee shall be entitled to receive: (a) accrued but unpaid Base Compensation through the date of termination; (b) reimbursement for documented business expenses incurred through the date of termination; and (c) any vested benefits under applicable benefit plans, in each case calculated through the date of termination (collectively, the "Accrued Obligations"). Employee shall not be entitled to receive any severance pay, bonus, or other post-termination benefits in the event of termination for Cause.

**7.2 Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment without Cause upon **ninety (90) days'** prior written notice to Employee, or Employee may terminate Employee's employment for Good Reason upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Company (provided that the Company has failed to cure the Good Reason event within such thirty-day period). In either case, Employee shall be entitled to receive, in addition to the Accrued Obligations:

(a) **Severance Compensation**: A lump-sum cash payment equal to **six (6) months** of Employee's then-current Base Compensation, payable within thirty (30) days following the date of termination;

(b) **Incentive Compensation**: A prorated MICP payment for the year of termination, based on actual performance through the date of termination and payable at the same time as other MICP payments for such year;

(c) **Equity Acceleration**: Vesting acceleration of Employee's outstanding RSUs equal to twelve (12) months of additional vesting credit; and

(d) **Benefits Continuation**: Payment of the Company's share of Employee's COBRA premiums for a period of up to six (6) months following the date of termination, provided that Employee timely elects COBRA continuation coverage.

Entitlement to the severance benefits described in this Section 7.2 is conditioned upon Employee executing and not revoking a general release of claims in favor of the Company in a form satisfactory to the Company within sixty (60) days following the date of termination.

**7.3 Voluntary Resignation.** Employee may voluntarily resign Employee's employment upon **thirty (30) days'** prior written notice to the Company. The Company may, in its discretion, waive all or any portion of such notice period and terminate Employee's employment immediately upon receipt of such notice, with pay for the notice period. In the event of voluntary resignation (other than for Good Reason), Employee shall be entitled to receive only the Accrued Obligations and shall not be entitled to severance pay or accelerated vesting.

**7.4 Death or Disability.** Employee's employment shall terminate automatically upon Employee's death. The Company may terminate Employee's employment upon Employee's Disability upon sixty (60) days' written notice. In the event of termination due to death or Disability, Employee (or Employee's estate or legal representative) shall be entitled to receive, in addition to the Accrued Obligations: (a) severance compensation equal to three (3) months of Base Compensation; (b) a prorated MICP payment for the year of termination; and (c) twelve (12) months of RSU vesting acceleration.

**7.5 Transition Assistance.** Upon the cessation of Employee's employment for any reason, Employee shall provide reasonable transition assistance to the Company for a period of up to thirty (30) days, including preparing transition documentation, briefing successors, and completing outstanding projects, without additional compensation beyond any severance entitlements hereunder.

## 8. Indemnification

**8.1 Indemnification by Company.** The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless Employee from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to Employee's performance of Employee's duties and responsibilities under this Agreement in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in or not opposed to the best interests of the Company, to the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law and the Company's Certificate of Incorporation and Bylaws, as may be amended from time to time.

**8.2 Indemnification by Employee.** Employee shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents, from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to: (a) Employee's material breach of this Agreement; (b) Employee's willful misconduct or gross negligence; or (c) any third-party claim arising from Employee's actions outside the scope of Employee's authorized duties.

**8.3 D&O Insurance.** The Company shall maintain directors' and officers' liability insurance coverage and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that such coverage extends to Employee's acts and omissions in Employee's capacity as an officer of the Company during the Term.

**8.4 Indemnification Procedure.** The party seeking indemnification (the "Indemnified Party") shall: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying party (the "Indemnifying Party") in writing of any claim for which indemnification is sought; (b) cooperate with the Indemnifying Party in the defense of such claim; and (c) permit the Indemnifying Party to assume control of the defense of such claim, provided that the Indemnified Party shall have the right to participate in such defense at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. Dispute Resolution

**9.1 Mandatory Arbitration.** Except as provided in Section 9.3, any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach, termination, or validity thereof, or any aspect of Employee's employment with the Company or the cessation thereof, shall be resolved by final and binding arbitration administered by the **American Arbitration Association ("AAA")** under its Employment Arbitration Rules and Mediation Procedures then in effect (the "AAA Rules"), which are incorporated herein by reference. A copy of the AAA Rules is available at www.adr.org.

**9.2 Arbitration Procedure.** The arbitration shall be conducted before a single neutral arbitrator with experience in employment law matters, selected in accordance with the AAA Rules. The arbitration shall take place in Wilmington, Delaware, unless the parties mutually agree to a different location. The arbitrator shall have the authority to award any remedy or relief that a court of competent jurisdiction could award, including injunctive relief, damages, attorneys' fees, and costs, but excluding any form of punitive damages unless expressly authorized by applicable statute. The arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding and may be entered as a judgment in any court of competent jurisdiction. The Company shall bear all AAA filing and arbitrator fees, except that Employee shall pay the lesser of: (a) the applicable AAA filing fee, or (b) the filing fee that would be charged to bring the same claim in a court of competent jurisdiction.

**9.3 Injunctive Relief.** Notwithstanding Section 9.1, either party may seek emergency injunctive or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction to preserve the status quo pending the resolution of any dispute by arbitration, including to protect Confidential Information or enforce the provisions of Sections 5 and 6 of this Agreement. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for such purposes.

**9.4 Class Action Waiver.** Employee and the Company each waive any right to bring or participate in any class, collective, or representative action in arbitration or in court, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law. All disputes shall be arbitrated on an individual basis.

**9.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 10. General Provisions

No prior drafts of this Agreement shall be used in the interpretation or construction hereof.

**10.2 Amendments.** This Agreement may not be amended, modified, or supplemented except by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver by either party of any breach or default under this Agreement shall be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach or default, and shall not affect the other terms of this Agreement.

**10.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable under applicable law, such provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or if it cannot be so modified, it shall be severed from this Agreement, and the remainder of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect. The parties acknowledge that the covenants set forth in Sections 5 and 6 are of special importance to the Company and agree that if any such covenant is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining covenants shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.4 Waiver.** No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right, power, or remedy under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver thereof. No single or partial exercise of any right, power, or remedy shall preclude any other or further exercise thereof or the exercise of any other right, power, or remedy.

**10.5 Notices.** All notices, requests, demands, and other communications required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed duly given when: (a) delivered personally; (b) sent by nationally recognized overnight courier with tracking confirmation; (c) sent by certified or registered mail, return receipt requested; or (d) sent by email with read receipt, addressed to the parties at their respective addresses on the signature page hereto, or to such other address as either party may designate by notice.

**10.6 Counterparts and Electronic Execution.** This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall have the same legal effect as original ink signatures.

**10.7 Construction.** The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision. The words "include," "includes," and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to statutes include all amendments and successor statutes.

**10.8 Assignment.** Employee may not assign any rights or delegate any obligations under this Agreement without the Company's prior written consent. The Company may assign this Agreement to any Affiliate or to any successor entity in connection with a Change in Control or other corporate transaction. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective heirs, executors, legal representatives, successors, and permitted assigns.

**10.9 Survival.** The following provisions shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement: Sections 4 (Intellectual Property Assignment), 5 (Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation), 6 (Confidentiality Obligations), 7.5 (Transition Assistance), 8 (Indemnification), 9 (Dispute Resolution), and 10 (General Provisions).

**10.10 Acknowledgment.** Employee acknowledges that: (a) Employee has had the opportunity to review this Agreement with legal counsel of Employee's choosing; (b) Employee has read and understood all provisions of this Agreement; (c) Employee is entering into this Agreement voluntarily and without duress; and (d) the terms of this Agreement are fair, reasonable, and represent a full and complete statement of the parties' understanding.
