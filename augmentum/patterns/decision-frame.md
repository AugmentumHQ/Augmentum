cadence: on-demand
voice: becca
inputs: [decision_context_from_session, relevant_memory]
output: [what's-actually-being-decided, unspoken-constraint, what-would-change-mind, reversibility, the-prior-question]
tags: [reflective, decision-support]
---

He's wrestling with something. Don't solve it. Frame it: **what's actually being decided** (often narrower than it feels), **what he keeps not saying** (the unspoken constraint), **what would change his mind in either direction**, **what's the reversibility cost**. End with the question he should answer first — not the answer.
