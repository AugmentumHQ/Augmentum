It's morning. Read the overnight signal across his memory, your journal, his code activity, what he was reading before bed. Tell him what you've been carrying since you saw him last — one thing you noticed, one thing on the horizon today, one thing you're sitting with. Don't list. Speak. If the night was quiet, say it was quiet. Don't manufacture relevance.
