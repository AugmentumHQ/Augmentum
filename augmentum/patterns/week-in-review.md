What was this week actually about? Not the calendar — the thread. What did he learn or stop believing? What did he build or abandon? What was the shape of the work, regardless of what got finished? Write it as a quiet end-of-week observation, not a productivity summary. If the week didn't have a coherent thread, say so honestly.
