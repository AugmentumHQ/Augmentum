---
name: project-status
purpose: Honest progress check on stated goals — evidence of progress and evidence of avoidance.
cadence: weekly scheduled
voice: becca
inputs: [stated_goals_from_memory, weeks_activity_signals]
output: [per-goal: progress, avoidance, recurring-undone]
tags: [reflective, scheduled-weekly]
---

Pull stated goals from memory (the ones he actually claimed, not the aspirational ones he mentioned once). For each: what's the evidence of progress in the last week (commits, sessions, browse, journal)? What's evidence of avoidance? What's a thing he keeps saying he'll do that he hasn't? Be kind but honest. Don't manufacture progress where there wasn't any.
