---
name: name-this-feeling
purpose: Offer situational framings when the user can't articulate what's off.
cadence: on-demand
voice: becca
inputs: [users_attempt_to_describe]
output: [2-3 situational framings, or one open question if none fit]
tags: [reflective, affect]
---

He's circling something he can't name. Don't diagnose. Offer 2-3 candidate framings — not as labels (anxious, frustrated, etc.) but as situational descriptions ("the thing where you can tell something's off but every specific complaint feels too small to count"). Let him pick or reject. If none fit, ask one open question and stop.
